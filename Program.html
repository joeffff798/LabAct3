Degree Programs at UST CICS

Discover the undergraduate programs offered by the College of Information and Computing Sciences.

Our Programs

Duration: 4 Years

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

BSCS

The BSCS program equips students with strong foundations in computing theory, mathematics, algorithm design, and software engineering. Graduates are prepared for careers in research, software development, and advanced computing fields.

Duration: 4 Years

Bachelor of Science in Information Systems

BSIS

The BSIS program combines IT knowledge with business concepts. Students learn systems analysis, enterprise resource planning, and data management to bridge the gap between technology and organizational needs.

Duration: 4 Years

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology

BSIT

The BSIT program covers web development, network administration, database management, and IT project management. It trains students to apply technology solutions in real-world settings.

Academic Departments

💻

Computer Science

Focuses on the theory, math, and creation of computing technologies, designing algorithms, developing software from scratch, and advancing fields like AI.

📊

Information Systems

Serves as the bridge between technology and business, focusing on how organizations use data, systems, and technology to achieve operational goals.

🔧

Information Technology

Focuses on the practical implementation and maintenance of computing systems, managing hardware, network infrastructure, databases, and cybersecurity to keep systems running.

Recognitions & Accreditations
