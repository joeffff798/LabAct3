Patron Saint

St. Vincent Ferrer

St. Vincent Ferrer, O.P. (1350–1419) was a Dominican friar renowned for his preaching and miraculous works. He is the patron saint of the UST College of Information and Computing Sciences, guiding students in their pursuit of knowledge and service.

His connection to CICS reflects the college's commitment to integrating Christian values with academic and technological excellence.

Feast Day: April 5
